# Source sheet status

The wine sheets in `flights/` are the working reference for the site. See `VERIFICATION.md` for the separate online-verification audit.

The original photographs were supplied separately and **are not stored in this public repository**. Their filenames are intentionally omitted here because they are not accessible through the repo.

A handwritten **O** on a source sheet means it is **old/outdated**. Do not use it to overwrite current wine information without confirmation.

## Menu-based reconstructions

Da Vines Picpoul Blanc, Cove Mesa Viognier, Laramita Pinot Noir, and Laramita Sagrantino now have tasting profiles reconstructed from Art of Wine's published menu (https://artofwinesedona.com/sedona-the-art-of-wine-food-menu). The original sheets remain missing or outdated, and the exact vintages and technical specifications still need confirmation.
