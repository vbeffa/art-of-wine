# Source sheet status

The wine sheets in `flights/` are the working reference for the site. See `VERIFICATION.md` for the separate online-verification audit.

The original photographs were supplied separately and **are not stored in this public repository**. Their filenames are intentionally omitted here because they are not accessible through the repo.

**Current-menu authority:** A supplied photograph **without** a handwritten `O` is a current tasting sheet. A photograph **with** a handwritten `O` is old/outdated. The restaurant's online menu may lag behind the current printed sheets; never replace an unmarked photographed wine, vintage, or technical detail because the online menu differs. Use online sources primarily for missing or O-marked sheets.

## Menu-based reconstructions

Da Vines Picpoul Blanc, Cove Mesa Viognier, Laramita Pinot Noir, and Laramita Sagrantino now have tasting profiles reconstructed from Art of Wine's published menu (https://artofwinesedona.com/sedona-the-art-of-wine-food-menu). The original sheets remain missing or outdated, and the exact vintages and technical specifications still need confirmation.

## October 15, 2026 menu and bottle photographs

A subsequent set of 15 photographs includes current printed menus and front/back bottle lineups for Arizona White, Arizona Red, Worldly White, Worldly Red, and Ultimate Red. The photographed menu and bottle lineups identify Marqués de Riscal Rioja (instead of Arienzo), Château Livran Médoc (instead of Château Tertre de Courban), and Troupis 2024 Fteri Agiorgitiko (instead of Le Mentor Gigondas). Those three index entries were updated, with the earlier sheets retained in the repository.

The printed menu lists the Worldly White rosé as a rotating dry rosé; the photographed bottle is Scaia. It also lists 2020 Avignonesi and 2023 Terroir Cabernet Sauvignon, which differ from older sheet filenames. Keep vintage differences visible until the source sheets are reconciled. The Sweet Wine and Mead menu confirms Carl Reh Sweet Red is still listed.

Bottle photographs were optimized into a separate ZIP for review. The binary images are not yet committed to the repository; the site continues to use external images until those files can be uploaded.
