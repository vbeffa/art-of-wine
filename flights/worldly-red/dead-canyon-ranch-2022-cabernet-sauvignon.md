# Dead Canyon Ranch Cabernet Sauvignon

**Bottle image:** https://www.hamptonswineshoppe.com/cdn/shop/files/2022MercerEstatesDeadCanyonRanchCabernetSauvignon.jpg?v=1750435953  

**Flight:** worldly red  
**Vintage:** 2022  
**Origin:** Horse Heaven Hills, Washington, USA  
**Grapes:** 81% Cabernet Sauvignon, 10% Malbec, 8% Syrah, 1% Merlot  
**Alcohol:** 14.5%

## Tasting profile
Cherry, cranberry, raspberry, plum, anise, vanilla, white pepper.

## Winemaking and vineyard
Stainless-steel fermentation; 20% new French and American oak; aged 18 months.

## Food pairing and technical details
TA: 0.55 g/100 mL; pH: 3.78; UPC: 898012002804.

## Restaurant menu tasting notes
Blackberry and mint aromas; black cherry, cassis, baking spice, earth, vanilla, and oak.
