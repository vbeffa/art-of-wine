# Arienzo de Marqués de Riscal Crianza

**Flight:** worldly red  
**Vintage:** 2022  
**Origin:** Rioja Alavesa, Rioja DOCa, Spain  
**Grapes:** 100% Tempranillo  
**Alcohol:** Approximately 14%

## Tasting profile
Cherry, raspberry, red plum, vanilla, coconut, cedar, baking spice; balanced tannins.

## Winemaking and vineyard
Approximately 17 months in American oak, then bottle aging.

## Food pairing and technical details
Pairings: jamón ibérico, Manchego, chorizo, lamb, roast chicken, mushrooms. Serve 16-18°C; drink through 2030.
