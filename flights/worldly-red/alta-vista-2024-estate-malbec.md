# Alta Vista Estate Malbec

**Flight:** worldly red  
**Vintage:** 2024  
**Origin:** Mendoza, Argentina  
**Grapes:** 100% Malbec  
**Alcohol:** 14%

## Tasting profile
Black berries, cherry, violet, mint, spice and vanilla; full-bodied with firm tannins.

## Winemaking and vineyard
50% aged 12 months in French oak; 50% in stainless steel and concrete; four months bottle aging.

## Food pairing and technical details
pH: 3.75; acidity: 5.1 g/L. Serve at 60°F.
