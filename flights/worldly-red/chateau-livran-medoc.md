# Château Livran Médoc

**Flight:** Worldly Red

## Tasting Notes
Blackcurrant, black cherry, and fig aromas. Concentrated raspberry, mulberry, and cassis on the palate. Structured tannins and a long finish of cool mint, clove, and tobacco.
