# Pike Road Pinot Noir

**Flight:** worldly red  
**Vintage:** 2024  
**Origin:** Willamette Valley, Oregon, USA  
**Grapes:** 100% Pinot Noir  
**Alcohol:** 13.5%

## Tasting profile
Fruit-forward, concentrated, complex.

## Winemaking and vineyard
Hand sorted; stainless-steel fermented; aged in French oak and stainless steel up to 8 months.

## Food pairing and technical details
Cases: 39,817; TA: 5.5; pH: 3.62.
