# Pierre Le Grand Crozes-Hermitage Blanc

**Flight:** ultimate white  
**Vintage:** 2022  
**Origin:** Rhône, France  
**Grapes:** 60% Marsanne, 40% Roussanne

## Tasting profile
Peach, pineapple, white flowers, orchard and tropical fruit, citrus, mineral finish.

## Winemaking and vineyard
Marsanne and Roussanne blend.
