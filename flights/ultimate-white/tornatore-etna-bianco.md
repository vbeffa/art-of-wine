# Tornatore Etna Bianco

**Flight:** ultimate white  
**Vintage:** Not specified  
**Origin:** Etna, Sicily, Italy  
**Grapes:** Carricante  
**Alcohol:** Not specified

## Tasting profile
Green apple, grapefruit, white peach, aromatic herbs, minerality.

## Winemaking and vineyard
Hand harvested, fermented at 15-16°C; three months on fine lees.

## Food pairing and technical details
Northern slope of Etna; harvest second week of October.

## Restaurant menu tasting notes
Lemon zest, green apple, white peach, wild herbs, crushed stone, and subtle salinity.
