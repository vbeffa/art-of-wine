# Escarpment Martinborough Sauvignon Blanc

**Flight:** ultimate white  
**Vintage:** 2024  
**Origin:** Martinborough, New Zealand  
**Grapes:** Sauvignon Blanc  
**Alcohol:** 13%

## Tasting profile
Gooseberry, nettle, guava, white flowers, lime, mineral finish.

## Winemaking and vineyard
Stainless steel and 20% French oak puncheons; 2% new oak; six months aging.

## Food pairing and technical details
TA: 7.2; pH: 2.92; residual sugar: 2.8 g/L.
