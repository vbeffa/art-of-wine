# Ca' dei Frati I Frati Lugana DOC

**Flight:** ultimate white  
**Vintage:** Not specified  
**Origin:** Lombardy, Italy  
**Grapes:** 100% Turbiana  
**Alcohol:** Not specified

## Tasting profile
White balsamic flowers, apricot and almond; fresh, linear and elegant.

## Winemaking and vineyard
Steel vinification; no malolactic fermentation; at least six months on fine lees, two months in bottle.

## Food pairing and technical details
Pairings: delicate starters, fish. Soil: limestone and clay.

## Restaurant menu tasting notes
Delicate white flowers, balsamic notes, and apricot in a fresh, elegant wine.
