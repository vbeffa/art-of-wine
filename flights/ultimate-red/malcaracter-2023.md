# Malcaracter

**Flight:** ultimate red  
**Vintage:** 2023  
**Origin:** Ribera del Duero, Spain  
**Grapes:** 100% Tempranillo  
**Alcohol:** Not specified

## Tasting profile
Raspberry, cherry, violet, spice, cedar, earth; smooth tannins.

## Winemaking and vineyard
Three months in oak and three months in concrete vats; spontaneous fermentation.

## Food pairing and technical details
Organic; 8,000 bottles; vineyards at 900-1,000 m.
