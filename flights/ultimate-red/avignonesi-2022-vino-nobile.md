# Avignonesi Vino Nobile di Montepulciano DOCG

**Bottle image:** https://www.wineandcheeseplace.com/cdn/shop/files/AVIGNONESIVINONOBLEDIMONT.jpg?v=1772287355  

**Flight:** ultimate red  
**Vintage:** 2022  
**Origin:** Tuscany, Italy  
**Grapes:** 100% Sangiovese  
**Alcohol:** 14%

## Tasting profile
Red berries, pomegranate, violet, vanilla, cherry, plum, licorice; medium body and fine tannins.

## Winemaking and vineyard
20-25 days maceration; 18 months in large casks and tonneaux.

## Food pairing and technical details
233,306 bottles; source lists James Suckling 95 points.

## Restaurant menu tasting notes
Cherries, pomegranate, tomato leaf and vanilla with medium body and smooth tannins. The restaurant menu lists the 2020 vintage, not the 2022 vintage in this entry.

Source: https://artofwinesedona.com/sedona-the-art-of-wine-food-menu
