# Avignonesi Vino Nobile di Montepulciano DOCG

**Flight:** ultimate red  
**Vintage:** 2022  
**Origin:** Tuscany, Italy  
**Grapes:** 100% Sangiovese  
**Alcohol:** 14%

## Tasting profile
Red berries, pomegranate, violet, vanilla, cherry, plum, licorice; medium body and fine tannins.

## Winemaking and vineyard
20-25 days maceration; 18 months in large casks and tonneaux.

## Food pairing and technical details
233,306 bottles; source lists James Suckling 95 points.
