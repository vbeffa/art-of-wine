# Château Gaby Grand Vin de Bordeaux

**Flight:** ultimate red  
**Vintage:** 2015  
**Origin:** Canon-Fronsac, Bordeaux, France  
**Grapes:** 80% Merlot, 10% Cabernet Franc, 10% Cabernet Sauvignon  
**Alcohol:** Not specified

## Tasting profile
Dark black fruit, richness and lively acidity.

## Winemaking and vineyard
Traditional cement vats; 12-18 months in French oak, 55% new.

## Food pairing and technical details
Source cites Wine Enthusiast 90 points.
