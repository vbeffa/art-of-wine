# Terroir Napa Valley Cabernet Sauvignon

**Flight:** ultimate red  
**Vintage:** 2022  
**Origin:** Napa Valley, California, USA  
**Grapes:** 88% Cabernet Sauvignon, 5% Petit Verdot, 4% Malbec, 3% Merlot  
**Alcohol:** 14.5%

## Tasting profile
Blackberry, cassis, chocolate, vanilla, dark cherry, plum and mocha.

## Winemaking and vineyard
22 months in French oak, 35% new.

## Food pairing and technical details
912 cases; UPC: 826453521011.

## Restaurant menu tasting notes
Black cherry, cassis, bramble, vanilla, tobacco, cocoa, black raspberry, chocolate, espresso and anise. The restaurant menu lists the 2023 vintage, not 2022.
