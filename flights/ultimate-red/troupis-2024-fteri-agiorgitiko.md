# Troupis 2024 Fteri Agiorgitiko

**Flight:** Ultimate Red

## Tasting Notes
A chillable Greek red with violet hue, blackcurrant and pomegranate aromas, red fruit, soft tannins, and a hint of spice. The photographed menu notes indigenous yeast.

Greece.
