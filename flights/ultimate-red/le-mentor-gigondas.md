# Le Mentor Gigondas

**Flight:** ultimate red  
**Vintage:** Not specified  
**Origin:** Gigondas, Rhône, France  
**Grapes:** Grenache, Syrah, Mourvèdre  
**Alcohol:** 14.5%

## Tasting profile
Plum, blueberry, blackberry, licorice, raisin, pepper and herbs.

## Winemaking and vineyard
Natural yeast; 30-day fermentation and maceration; concrete and old foudre.

## Food pairing and technical details
Pairings: lamb, duck confit, coq au vin. Decant at least one hour; cellaring 10-15 years.
