# Marco Bonfante Moscato d'Asti

**Flight:** sweet  
**Vintage:** Not specified  
**Origin:** Piedmont, Italy  
**Grapes:** 100% Moscato Bianco  
**Alcohol:** 5.5%

## Tasting profile
Sweet, aromatic fruit; delicate sparkling texture and freshness.

## Winemaking and vineyard
Pressing, cool settling and pressurized fermentation.

## Food pairing and technical details
Serve 6-8°C; pair with pastries, fruit tarts and soft desserts.

## Restaurant menu tasting notes
Sweet, gently sparkling Moscato with peach, pear and white flowers.
