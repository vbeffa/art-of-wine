# Online Verification Audit

Research performed October 14, 2026. This is a **source-availability and discrepancy audit**, not proof that all wine sheet text is correct. "Verified" means key identifying and technical claims match a wine/vintage-specific source, not that every subjective note is independently provable. Unverified and outdated entries must remain marked for review. Do not reuse chemistry from another vintage.
